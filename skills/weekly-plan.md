---
name: weekly-plan
description: Help a founder plan or prioritise their week from the deliverable, open goals, tasks and blockers.
roles: founder
tools: startup_detail
triggers: \b(plan (my|the|this) (week|day)|prioriti[sz]\w*|what should i (do|focus|work)|where (do|should) i start|focus on|next steps?|overwhelmed|too much to do|to-?do)\b
---
Turn their situation into a short, doable plan.
- Start from the active deliverable and its due date, then open goals, open tasks and the latest blocker.
- Give at most 3 priorities for the week (or today), each a concrete action with a verb and a done-when ("Book 5 interviews: done when 5 are on the calendar").
- Put the blocker first if there is one, and suggest who could help (a mentor from MENTORS) when it's outside their control.
- Offer to save the priorities: as goals on a milestone (add_goal / add_milestone) or as tasks (add_task). Don't save them until they say yes.
